Estudios de Mercado I · Tema 5 — Question checker worksheet

A leading question asked of 10,000 people is still leading. Train your eye on faulty questions first, then scan your own draft, tick the launch checklist and run an AI pretest. The scanner flags what may be wrong — you decide whether it is.

What this practical is for

To train your eye to spot faulty questions (5.3) and response biases (5.5), then pretest your own questionnaire before fieldwork (5.6–5.7) — because a bigger sample never fixes a bad question.

What you will do

  1. Step 1 — practise on 15 faulty questions. Aim for 12 or more, and read the rewrite for each one.10 min
  2. Step 2 — came from the builder? Your questionnaire, project topic and analysis plan are already here. If not, paste your questionnaire and write the topic. For every flag, decide: real fault or false alarm?15 min
  3. Step 3 — tick the launch checklist honestly. Every unticked box is a task still to do.5 min
  4. Step 4 — add your analysis plan and run the AI pretest (three passes). Compare it with your own diagnosis.10 min
  5. Step 5 — pilot with 3–5 real people, time it on a phone, and record every change with its reason. Download the PDF.20 min + pilot
What you hand in
  • The PDF: the flags you accepted or rejected, with reasons.
  • The checklist.
  • Your changes, final number of questions and timed length.
How it is marked
  • Rejecting a false alarm with a good reason counts as much as fixing a real fault.
  • The faults you fixed that the scanner missed.
  • Evidence of the pilot — what real people stumbled on.

1 Practise — what is wrong with this question?

Pick the fault. You get the reason and a clean rewrite straight away. The test for every question: could two reasonable people read it differently? and can someone answer honestly without being nudged?

2 Scan your own draft

Paste the questionnaire: one question per line, with its options on the lines below starting with - (or on the same line after a colon, separated by /). The builder's “Part B” output is already in this format.

3 The launch checklist (5.6)

Do not send it until every box is ticked. These are the checks no scanner can make for you.
